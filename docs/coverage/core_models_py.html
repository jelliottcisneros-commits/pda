34class UnverifiedUser(AbstractUser): 

35 """ 

36 This model will store user's information until their email is confirmed. 

37 Email is not unique for two reasons: 

38 - Someone other than the user tries to sign up with the user's email, but the user deletes the verification email 

39 since he/she didn't try registering. 

40 - The user registers, but forgets to verify the email or accidentally deletes it. 

41 In both cases, there should be no user with email already exists error on a subsequent tries. 

42 """

233 ''' 

234 If secondary_power_perspective is set, then we need to look at demographics. 

235 secondary_demographic_type tells us which of the fields under Demographic will we have to look at.

237 the secondary_power_perspective instead of the primary_power_pespective 

238 Example: 

239 primary_power_perspective: 'Strength' 

240 secondary_power_perspective: 'Appreciation' 

241 secondary_demographic_type: 'gender' 

242 secondary_demographic_choice: 'Male' 

243 Scenario 1: assessment.demographic.gender != 'Male' 

244 response.power_perspective = question.primary_power_perspective = Strength 

245 Scenario 2: assessment.demographic.gender == 'Male' 

246 response.power_perspective = question.secondary_power_perspective = Appreciation 

247 '''
