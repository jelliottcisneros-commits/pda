You will also receive an email with your results and related information about the Power of Difference Model (PDM). Please know that the graphs and data provided may not make a lot of sense until your conversation with your consultant—and can easily be misread!

Your highly trained consultant won't interpret your results for you. Instead, your consultant is trained to ask questions that support you in exploring what the results mean to you, where that meaning comes from in your life experience, and what direction you feel compelled to go based on what you discover.

We take this approach because we have found that identical results can mean very different things for different people. The Power of Difference Assessment (PDA) is really a supported conversation starter.

Your consultant will have lots of experience listening to these conversations and may offer salient questions or insights to support your exploration and learning.

Rather than thinking of the PDA as a skill or competency assessment—like a math test—think of it more like a personality assessment. There is no better or worse, no blame or shame, no smart or not smart. You decide the meaning of your results, with the support of your consultant.

If you have questions before or after your consultation, or if you don't see your results email in your inbox or spam folder, please contact us at info@thesum.org.
