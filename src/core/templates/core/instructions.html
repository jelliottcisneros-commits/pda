DIRECTIONS: For each numbered item below, place the letter (a, b, c, d, e) that best matches your level of agreement. Answer as honestly as possible. NOTES:

  • LGBQ+ = Lesbian, Gay, Bi-Sexual, Queer and/or Questioning--the "+" represents other identities: pansexual, androgynous, asexual, demisexual.
